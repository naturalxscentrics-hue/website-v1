Beginner checklist

Before you add anything new, give your skin room to respond.

  • Patch test each new product before broad use
  • Introduce one product at a time
  • Follow the product directions instead of overusing more product
  • Pause use if irritation occurs
  • Speak with a qualified professional for persistent skin concerns
