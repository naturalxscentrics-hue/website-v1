Keep it practical

A good grooming routine should not feel like homework.

  • Keep beard oil near the products you already use
  • Apply after cleansing or grooming when it fits your schedule
  • Use a consistent amount so the feel is predictable
  • Patch test before broad use, especially when trying a new formula
