About the brand

Natural XScentrics was born from Tiffany Palmer's pain, purpose, science, and faith.

Natural XScentrics is a founder-driven skincare company built to make gentler care feel accessible, intentional, and deeply personal.

What started as a childhood battle with painful skin issues grew into a calling to create products and experiences that help women and families feel more comfortable, more confident, and more supported in their skin.

Founder story

Built from lived pain, a mother's turning point, and a chemist's understanding of ingredients.

As a child, Tiffany lived with a severe skin condition that caused painful outbreaks and open sores on the palms of her hands and the bottoms of her feet. There were many doctor's visits, many questions, and a lot of trial and error, but not many clear answers.

  • In 2017, after her daughter was born, Tiffany saw signs that her daughter might face some of the same skin struggles.
  • That moment led her back into research, drawing on her chemistry background and deeper ingredient knowledge.
  • Natural XScentrics grew from the desire to offer gentler, more intentional care rooted in real-life skin needs.

Natural XScentrics is more than a skincare brand to me. It is a reminder that purpose can come from pain, that faith requires movement, and that resilience is built one step at a time.

Tiffany Palmer
