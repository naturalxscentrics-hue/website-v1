About the brand

Natural XScentrics was born from pain, purpose, science, and faith.

Natural XScentrics is a founder-driven skincare company built to make gentler care feel accessible, intentional, and deeply personal.

What started as a childhood struggle with difficult skin concerns grew into a calling to create products and experiences that help women and families feel more comfortable, more confident, and more supported in their skin.

Natural XScentrics is more than a skincare brand to me. It is a reminder that purpose can come from pain, that faith requires movement, and that resilience is built one step at a time.

Tiffany Palmer

Founder story

Built from lived pain, a mother's turning point, and a chemist's understanding of ingredients.

As a child, Tiffany lived through painful skin challenges on the palms of her hands and the bottoms of her feet. There were many doctor's visits, many questions, and a lot of trial and error, but not many clear answers.

  • In 2017, after her daughter was born, Tiffany saw signs that her daughter might face some of the same skin struggles.
  • That moment led her back into research, drawing on her chemistry background and deeper ingredient knowledge.
  • Natural XScentrics grew from the desire to offer gentler, more intentional care rooted in real-life skin needs.

Behind the Rainbow

The rainbow is a personal reminder of God's promise, presence, and protection.

In scripture, the rainbow appears after Noah and the flood as God's promise that He would never flood the earth again. For Tiffany, that promise became personal: no matter how low life gets, God still has her back.

That is why the rainbow matters to Natural XScentrics. It is not just a colorful brand detail. It is a sign of faith, survival, and the belief that even after hard seasons, there is still purpose, beauty, and covering.
