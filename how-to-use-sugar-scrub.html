Sugar scrub guide

How to use sugar scrub without overcomplicating body care.

Sugar scrub is an occasional body-care step that can help your skin feel smoother and make the rest of your routine feel more intentional.

Shop scrub pairings Choose moisture

Simple steps

Keep the scrub step gentle, occasional, and followed by moisture.

Step 1

Start with product directions

Use the scrub as directed and avoid areas where scrub is not appropriate. Gentle application matters more than pressure.

Step 2

Rinse and cleanse

Rinse well and keep the rest of the routine simple so your skin is not overwhelmed by too many steps at once.

Step 3

Finish with body butter

Follow with body butter for a soft-feeling finish and a care moment that feels complete from scrub to scent.

Avoid overuse

More pressure does not make the routine better.

  • Use sugar scrub as an occasional body-care step
  • Do not scrub irritated or uncomfortable skin
  • Patch test when trying a new scent or formula
  • Pause use if irritation occurs

Pair it well

Scrub, cleanse, moisturize, repeat with care.

  • Explore cleansing bars
  • Shop body butters
  • Build a full body care routine
